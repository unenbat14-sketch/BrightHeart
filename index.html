Organised volunteering

Charities and youth organisations give you training, a team and a clear role. This is a good choice if you want to commit for a longer time.

🚑

Humanitarian charities

Groups such as the Red Cross and Red Crescent train volunteers in first aid and help people in emergencies.

🏕️

Youth organisations

Scouts, Guides and similar clubs build confidence and leadership, and often run community projects.

🐾

Animal and nature groups

Shelters and conservation charities need help with care, cleaning and planting.

How to join

  1. Choose a cause you care about, such as animals, health, education or the environment.
  2. Research organisations online and check that they are trusted and registered.
  3. Contact them and ask about age limits, training and how many hours they need.
  4. Start small, then take on more once you feel confident.

Tip: Ask a parent, teacher or guardian to help you with your first message. Many organisations need their permission for under-18s.

Local volunteering

You don’t have to go far to help. Many chances are in your school, your street and your town.

🌳

Clean up your area

Organise a litter pick or plant a community garden with friends.

📚

Tutor younger students

Share what you know. Reading with a younger child can change their year.

🧓

Support older neighbours

Carry shopping, share a chat or help someone learn to use their phone.

🥫

Help a food bank

Collect, sort and hand out food for families who need it.

🎪

Run a school event

Bake sales, sponsored walks and talent shows raise money for local charities.

🤝

Welcome new students

Show new classmates around. It costs nothing and means a lot.

Try this week: Pick one idea, ask one friend to join you and set a date. Small and regular is better than big and never.

International ideas

Problems like hunger, climate change and lack of education affect people all over the world. There are many ways to help, even when you’re far away.

💻

Volunteer online

Translate texts, design posters or tutor students in other countries from your own computer.

💸

Raise money

Organise a fundraiser for an international charity that provides clean water, food or schooling.

🌍

Volunteer abroad

When you are older, you can join projects overseas, such as teaching, building or conservation.

📣

Spread awareness

Write, post and talk about global issues so more people understand and act.

Choose with care

Not every overseas project helps local people. Before you join or donate, check these things:

  1. Is the organisation registered, and does it share where its money goes?
  2. Do local people lead the project and decide what they need?
  3. Will the work still help after you leave?

Think globally, act locally: The best international idea might start with a poster in your school corridor.
